Having read aps-210.pdf (1 July 2025), apg-210.html, and ars-210.0.pdf (all 7 ARF 210 reporting forms and instructions) from scratch—without touching any code—here is a first-
  principles analysis of what the APS 210 problem actually is, what a bank is really optimising for, and the strongest possible stack (including a purpose-built language/IR) to
  make it bulletproof.
  ──────
  ## 1. What APS 210 Actually Is (Mathematically & Structurally)
  Superficially, APS 210 looks like weighted sums (HQLA/NCO, ASF/RSF, MLH/Liabilities). In reality, it is a coupled, non-linear, bitemporal, conservation-constrained resource
  allocation and simulation problem over a bank’s entire balance-sheet graph. The real complexity lives in seven structural properties:
  1. Multiple coupled regimes over one balance sheet, with deliberate asymmetries
      • Valuation basis diverges: LCR & MLH use Market Value (with LCR adjusting MV for early close-out cost of price-risk hedges, ars-210.0.pdf p. 39); NSFR uses Accounting
      Carrying Value net of specific provisions and unincluded GRCL, before capital deductions.
      • Securitisation scope diverges (aps-210.pdf §5): APS 120 capital-relief SPVs are excluded from MLH & NSFR, but included in LCR, Contractual Maturity Mismatch (ARF 210.3.1/3.
      2), 3-Year Funding Plan (ARF 210.4), and Daily Liquidity (ARF 210.5).
      • Maturity conventions invert: Liabilities use earliest possible redemption/option date; assets use latest possible maturity/extension option; amortising instruments split
      cashflows across <6m, 6m–1y, ≥1y buckets (and 14 buckets in ARF 210.3.x).
      • Consolidation & currency asymmetries: Level 1 vs Level 2 (intragroup elimination + cross-border trapped-surplus exclusion in strict HQLA1 → HQLA2A → HQLA2B → ALA waterfall
      order); AUD-only LCR (ARF 210.1B) has special FX derivative carve-outs (gross for funding transformation, excluded for trading/market-making).
  2. Counterfactual state transitions & coupled caps
      • 30-day unwind (Att A §§7–8): You do not cap today's HQLA directly—you simulate unwinding maturing (≤30d) HQLA↔HQLA secured funding, secured lending, and collateral swaps,
      compute the 15% HQLA2B cap and 40% HQLA2 cap on the unwound stock, and subtract the resulting cap adjustments from the unadjusted stock.
      • Coupled ratio constraints:
          • NCO = Outflows + APRA_Overlay − min(Inflows, 0.75 × Outflows)
          • Excess contractual lending obligations (ARF 210.1A Item 15.8) offset against implicit 50% inflow roll-over (max(obligations − implicit_rollover, 0)).
          • Maturing CLF securities only enter cash inflows (Item 21.2) to the extent they exceed the included CLF numerator cap.
          • Cross-ratio coupling: NSFR's 10% RSF quota for CLF/ALA assets (Att C §32, ARF 210.6 Items 26–27) is capped by LCR's included/approved CLF (ARF 210.1A Items 6.3 & 6.2.1),
          with residuals spilling into 65%/85% RSF; re-hypothecation ordering of owned vs borrowed securities must be consistent across LCR and NSFR (ars-210.0.pdf p. 150).
  3. Non-local entity-graph cliffs & hidden optimisation problems
      • You cannot classify a deposit row in isolation:
          • FCS $250k cap: Aggregated across all eligible accounts of a customer—splitting the $250k across accounts with different scorecard points (Table 1 & 2) changes run-off
          between 5%, 10%, and 25% (a per-customer knapsack allocation).
          • SME $2M cliff: Consolidated across the SME group; $1 above $2M flips the entire balance from retail (5%/10%/25% LCR; 95%/90% NSFR) to wholesale corporate (20%/40% LCR;
          50% NSFR).
          • Wholesale deposit insurance cliff: Unlike retail/operational deposits (which split at the cap), exceeding the limit by $1 flips the entire non-operational wholesale
          deposit from 20% to 40%.
          • Collateral pool encumbrance: Unallocated pools allow lowest-liquidity-first (LCR) and highest-RSF-first (NSFR) assignment—a genuine constrained allocation problem.
          • MLH cross-ADI netting (ARF 210.2 Items 9–12): Netting of placements by other ADIs against MLH deposits with other ADIs is aggregate across all ADIs (not bilateral) and
          reduces both numerator and denominator by min(placements_in, deposits_out).

  4. Strict conservation & no-double-counting laws
      • An asset in HQLA/CLF cannot also produce 30-day cash inflows (Att A §31).
      • Collateral posted as variation margin deducted from NSFR derivative liabilities cannot remain in RSF assets (Att C fn 12).
      • Every balance-sheet dollar must reconcile to the General Ledger under AASB, while "of which" / memo rows must never be summed into totals.
  5. Bitemporal & path-dependent state
      • 30-day downgrade grace period (Att A §25): Requires tracking exact downgrade dates vs reporting date.
      • 24-month rolling max absolute net 30-day collateral flow (Item 13.6): Daily collateral flow time-series netted by valid Master Netting Agreement.
      • 12-month monthly net outflows for trade finance & guarantees (Items 16.2–16.3, NSFR Items 52–53).
      • Fixed-term deposit pool tainting (Att A §42): Allowing one non-hardship early withdrawal despite a no-withdrawal clause taints the entire product category into demand
      deposits—an event-history invariant.
  6. Cross-standard dependencies: Directly consumes outputs/definitions from APS 110 (SFT/VM netting), APS 111 (capital & fair value), APS 112 (0%/20%/35% RW, LVR ≤ 80%, ECAI
  multi-rating rule), APS 113 (IRB PD-to-rating & SME fallback), APS 120 (securitisation relief), APS 220 (NPLs, provisions, GRCL), APS 221 (connected counterparties), and AASB 121
  (FX).
  7. Statutory discretions & crisis cadence: APRA can override almost any weight/overlay in writing (§71, Item 29.1), discretions do not auto-carry across standard revisions (§72),
  compliance is continuous daily (quarterly lowest/highest/mean EOD ratios), and ARF 210.5 demands same-day COB turnaround during stress.

  ## 2. What a Bank Actually Needs & Optimises For

  A bank does not merely want "a script that computes ARF 210 quarterly." Group Treasury, Risk, Finance, External Audit (APS 310), and APRA are simultaneously optimising five
  things:
  1. Minimising the Cost of the Liquidity Buffer (P&L / NIM)
      • Holding HQLA1 instead of loans costs a major bank $500M–$1.5B+/year in negative carry.
      • Coarse proxies, missing customer-level joins, suboptimal collateral/FCS allocation, and reporting volatility force banks to hold extra management buffers above 100%
      LCR/NSFR. Every unnecessary 1% of buffer or conservative data-quality fallback destroys tens of millions in earnings.
      • Funds & Liquidity Transfer Pricing (FTP/LTP, APS 210 §42): Treasury needs the exact marginal LCR and NSFR shadow price (∂LCR/∂x, ∂NSFR/∂x) of every business line, product,
      and large proposed trade pre-trade—including regime shifts when a cap (40% HQLA2, 75% inflow, CLF) binds.
  2. Zero-Error Regulatory & APS 310 Audit Survivability
      • Regulatory breaches trigger catastrophic APRA penalties (e.g., 10%+ NCO overlays under Item 29.1, costing billions in incremental liquid assets).
      • Under ARS 210.0 §§15–17, external auditors must test systems annually and an officer digitally signs the return. Auditors need GL-to-return completeness, zero unexplained
      Excel overlays, exact clause-to-contract lineage, and bitemporal replay (exact movement attribution: price vs FX vs maturity roll-down vs new trades vs rating vs rule change).
  3. Intraday & Crisis-Speed Agility
      • Modern bank runs happen in hours. Same-day ARF 210.5, rapid ELA (§70) data packs, daily EOD LCR/MLH, and intraday settlement liquidity (§41) are impossible on fragile
      overnight batch ETLs with manual spreadsheet adjustments.
  4. Unified Stress Testing & 3-Year Steering (§§62–66, ARF 210.4)
      • LCR is just one hard-coded 30-day scenario. Treasury must run dozens of idiosyncratic, market-wide, combined, 3-notch downgrade, 100%-notice-call, and protracted multi-
      horizon stress scenarios across Level 1, Level 2, currencies, and business lines—using the same contractual cashflow engine, not disconnected models.
  5. Safe Regulatory & Product Evolution
      • Standards evolve (2018 → 2022 → July 2025), products change (31-day notice savers, mortgage redraw), and APRA grants time-limited discretions. The bank needs executable,
      diffable, verifiable rules where documentation cannot drift from production code.

  ## 3. Why Conventional Stacks Fail
  • SQL / Spark / Warehouse Pipelines: Weak typing (NULLs, float drift, silent fan-out joins that double-count assets), brutal to express coupled unwinds/optimisations/cliffs,
  impossible to formally verify conservation laws or compute exact dual prices.
  • Vendor Black Boxes (QRM, Moody's, AxiomSL, OFSAA): Opaque GUI config, untestable/undiffable logic, slow batch runs, inevitably surrounded by unverified Excel "EUC" overlays
  where compliance failures breed.
  • Pure Proof Assistants Alone (Lean / Rocq / Isabelle): Unbeatable for proving theorems, unusable as a 100M-account sub-second incremental streaming engine or large-scale LP
  solver.
  • Pure Systems Code (Rust / Java / C++): Fast and strongly typed, but lacks conservation/linear-resource proofs across portfolios, lacks SMT exhaustiveness checking, lacks
  automatic cell-to-contract provenance, and mixes business policy with plumbing.

  ### A. Designing the Purpose-Built Language: Prudence

  Prudence is a total (always-terminating), pure, strongly-typed relational + algebraic + optimisation DSL whose core IR is tiny enough to embed directly in Lean 4 / SMT, yet
  expressive enough to compile to incremental dataflow and SQL/Arrow. Its six killer primitives map 1-to-1 to APS 210's failure modes:
  #### 1. Basis-, Currency-, and Convention-Indexed Exact Numeric Types

  • No IEEE-754 floats anywhere in core semantics—only exact rationals ℚ or exact 128-bit scaled decimals. Rounding (RoundToTenthMillion, RoundToWholeDollar) is a type-changing
  operator permitted only at the ReportingForm boundary.
  • Monetary types carry phantom dimensions: Money<Ccy, Basis> where Basis ∈ {MarketValue, HedgeAdjMV, RbaHaircutMV, CarryingNetProv, FaceValue}. Adding an LCR MarketValue to an
  NSFR CarryingNetProv, or adding USD to AUD without an explicit AASB121.fx_convert(rate, as_of), is a compile-time error.
  • Maturity types separate EarliestRedemptionDate (liabilities / ASF) from LatestMaturityDate (assets / RSF) so asymmetric option rules cannot be inverted.
  #### 2. Conservation Types & Linear Resource Partitioning (Eliminating Double-Counting Statically)

  • Balance-sheet and collateral holdings live in linearly-tracked Pool<T> collections.
  • Two built-in primitives enforce mass balance by construction:
      • partition pool by rules: The SMT checker proves exhaustiveness (no contract dropped) and disjointness—or if rules intentionally overlap, forces an explicit resolution
      policy like resolve_overlap: max_outflow_rate (directly encoding ars-210.0.pdf p. 45!).
      • split(amount, cap) -> (within, excess): Statically guarantees within + excess == amount, within >= 0, excess >= 0.
  • Linear capability consumption: Assigning a security lot to HQLA_Stock or CLF_Collateral consumes its 30dInflowRight, making it a type error to include that same lot in
  CashInflows (Att A §31). Similarly, deducting posted variation margin from NSFR derivative liabilities consumes its RSF_Asset token (Att C fn 12).
  • of_which / memo items are typed as read-only Observer<T>, which cannot be passed to sum_into_total(...).
  #### 3. First-Class Conservative Uncertainty Lattice (Uncertain<T>)

  This solves the #1 real-world bank problem—messy upstream data—without crashing EOD close or silently defaulting to optimistic false:
  • Every regulatory attribute (e.g., is_operational_excess_known, has_established_relationship, collateral_split_known, ultimate_owner_known) can carry Known(v) | Unknown(reason).
  • Because every regulatory rule has a well-defined prudential conservative direction (e.g., unknown operational excess → 100% non-operational; unknown secured/unsecured loan
  split → higher RSF; unknown bond holder → FI 0% ASF), the language propagates intervals [optimistic, prudential_conservative]:
      • Reported APRA number = prudential_conservative (100% compliant).
      • Data-Quality Capital Drag = prudential_conservative − optimistic, automatically priced via Treasury's FTP shadow price! Management gets a daily leaderboard: "Fixing missing
      SME group-link IDs on 412 accounts frees $680M of HQLA = $7.5M/year in NII."
  #### 4. Declarative Counterfactual Transitions (unwind and stress)

  Instead of hand-coding error-prone spreadsheet arithmetic like ARF 210.1A Item 1.5.1 (11.1.2 + 11.7.2 + 19.1.1.1 + ... - 11.1.1 - ...), express the actual financial physics once:

  • Portfolio_Unwound = unwind_sft_and_swaps(Portfolio, horizon: 30d) (which executes cash/collateral leg settlement while preserving conservation).
  • In the proof kernel (Lean 4 / Z3), formally prove that classify_HQLA(Portfolio_Unwound) equals APRA's ARF 210.1A derived form equations for all valid portfolios!
  • The exact same state-transition language expresses Stress Testing (apply_scenario(Portfolio, SevereCombinedStress)): shock prices, fire 3-notch downgrade triggers, exercise in-
  the-money options, call 100% of 31-day notice deposits, freeze wholesale rollovers, and roll forward t = 0 … 365 days.
  #### 5. Certified Embedded Optimisation (optimize) + Automatic Shadow Pricing

  For the genuine optimisation problems embedded inside APS 210 (customer-level FCS $250k allocation, unallocated collateral pool assignment, cross-ratio LCR/NSFR re-hypothecation
  consistency, and Treasury buffer/funding optimisation):
  • Prudence provides declarative optimize { minimize ... subject to ... } blocks compiled to an external solver (e.g., HiGHS / Clarabel / exact rational simplex).
  • The solver is NOT trusted: it returns both a primal allocation x^* and dual multipliers y^*, which are verified in O(n) by a tiny formally verified Primal-Dual Certificate
  Checker.
  • Huge economic win: The verified dual variables y^* (plus exact piecewise-linear subgradients at cap boundaries) are directly exported as the bank's APS 210 §42 Funds &
  Liquidity Transfer Prices (FTP/LTP)—telling every desk the true marginal cost of liquidity when a trade pushes the bank closer to the 40% HQLA2 cap, 75% inflow cap, or AUD LCR
  floor.

  #### 6. Mandatory Legal Provenance, Bitemporal Events, and Signed Overlays
  • Every rule block carries @citation, @effective, @jurisdiction metadata.
  • Evaluating the IR over a Provenance Semiring (instead of just scalar dollars) produces exact bi-directional lineage: click any cell in ARF 210.1A/210.6 → see the exact
  statutory clause, exact formula, contributing contracts, and per-contract decision witness; click any contract → see its exact impact across all 7 ARF forms.
  • No Excel overlays: APRA discretions (§71, Item 29.1, ARF 210.6 Items 18/46/54) and temporary data patches are typed, signed AST overlays with mandatory apra_instrument_ref,
  cryptographic executive sign-off, and hard expiry_date (automatically enforcing §72 when standards update).

  #### Sketch: What Prudence Looks Like in Practice

    @standard("APS 210", version: "2025-07-01", clause: "Att A §§34–46; APG 210 §§106–114")
    module RetailAndSmeOutflows(jurisdiction: AU_APRA) {

      // 1. Customer-group aggregation & $2M SME cliff (non-local graph rule)
      view CustomerTotals =
        group Deposits by customer_group_id {
          agg_bal = sum(balance : Money<AUD, CarryingNetProv>),
          is_tainted_td_pool = exists(event in EventLog | event.kind == NonHardshipEarlyWithdrawal
                                      && event.product_class == product_class) // Att A §42
        }

      // 2. Conservation-checked partition: Retail/Qualifying SME vs Wholesale Cliff
      partition (RetailEligible, WholesaleOverflow) =
        partition Deposits join CustomerTotals {
          case natural_person || smsf || qualifying_pie(agg_bal)         => RetailEligible
          case sme && managed_as_retail && agg_bal < 2_000_000.AUD       => RetailEligible
          otherwise                                                      => WholesaleOverflow
        }

      // 3. Per-customer optimal FCS $250k allocation (certified primal-dual LP/greedy)
      alloc fcs_alloc : ConservationSplit(RetailEligible, cap_per_customer: 250_000.AUD) =
        optimize per customer_id {
          minimize sum(d in customer_deposits |
                       weighted_outflow(d, fcs_alloc[d])
                     + weighted_outflow_uninsured(d, d.balance - fcs_alloc[d]))
          subject to
            sum(fcs_alloc[d]) <= min(250_000.AUD, sum(d.balance)),
            forall d: 0 <= fcs_alloc[d] <= d.balance
        }

      // 4. Scorecard with Uncertain<Bool> conservative fallback
      fn less_stable_rate(d: DepositSlice, is_above_fcs: Bool) -> Rate {
        let score = (if is_above_fcs then 2 else 0)
                  + (if conservative(d.is_online, worst: true) then 2 else 0)
                  + (if conservative(!d.has_est_relationship, worst: true) then 1 else 0)
                  + (if conservative(d.is_rate_driven, worst: true) then 1 else 0);
        if score >= 3 then 25% else 10%
      }

      // 5. Compile-time & runtime proof obligations
      invariant conservation:
        sum(RetailEligible.balance) + sum(WholesaleOverflow.balance) == sum(Deposits.balance)

      theorem monotonicity_sme_cliff:
        forall c: SmeCustomer, crossing_2m_threshold(c) ==> total_outflow_after >= total_outflow_before
    }

  ### C. Why This Design Is Uniquely Bulletproof (Key Architectural Decisions)

  1. Keep the Trusted Computing Base (TCB) Tiny: You never have to "trust" a giant distributed query engine, an optimising Rust compiler, or a numerical LP solver. You prove the
  specification in Lean 4/SMT, run continuous differential testing across Lean ↔ DBSP ↔ DuckDB on shrunk counterexamples, and verify every single production run through an
  independent, tiny Proof-Carrying Report Checker that validates conservation, caps, cross-form invariants, and primal-dual optimality on the concrete output before sign-off.
  2. Exact Move Attribution ("Explain") Built-In: Because the ledger is bitemporal and DBSP computes exact deltas, every morning Treasury gets an exact, zero-residual waterfall
  explaining why LCR/NSFR moved overnight (market price → FX → maturity roll-down crossing 30d/6m/1y boundaries → deposit flows → new trades → collateral moves → rating downgrades
  → rule/overlay changes).
  3. Clean Separation of Statutory Rules vs Behavioural Models: Statutory rules (APS 210 Attachments A–C) live in pure, deterministic, formally verified Prudence modules.
  Behavioural/statistical models (operational deposit excess estimation, mortgage prepayment curves for ARF 210.3.1 Item 10.5, stress run-off assumptions) are injected through
  explicit, versioned, Model-Risk-governed interfaces (APS 210 Att A §32) with full sensitivity analysis.
  4. Federated Multi-Jurisdiction Support: Offshore subsidiaries under Level 2 (e.g., NZ/RBNZ, UK/PRA, US/Fed) require host-supervisor retail/SME run-offs and ALA rules (Att A §26)
  alongside cross-border transferability caps (§29). Parameterising Prudence modules by JurisdictionPolicy lets one group engine run Level 1, Level 2, domestic books, and local
  host regulatory views cleanly without forked codebases.
  5. Where AI Fits (and Where It Must Never Go): Never put an LLM in the execution path. Use AI strictly offline as a regulatory-engineering copilot: ingesting new APRA
  determinations (like the July 2025 update) to draft diffs to Prudence specs, flagging semantic ambiguities between APS 210 prose and ARS 210.0 instructions, generating
  adversarial fuzz scenarios, and summarising verified move-attribution bridges for ALCO/Board packs—where every code change still has to pass the Lean/SMT proof checker and human
  sign-off.

  ### Bottom Line

  If you build APS 210 as a data pipeline, you get fragile reconciliations, opaque errors, and wasted HQLA carry. If you build it as a conservation-typed, formally verified domain
  language (Prudence) over a bitemporal canonical financial graph—compiling simultaneously to Lean 4/SMT (for proof), Rust + DBSP (for continuous daily/intraday/crisis speed),
  DuckDB/Arrow (for stress testing & audit), and certified LP optimisation (for collateral efficiency & FTP shadow pricing), gated by a tiny proof-carrying checker—you turn
  regulatory compliance from an expensive audit liability into a real-time, mathematically verified balance-sheet steering engine.*
